Good to Know

Booking Policy

We understand that plans can change. To ensure the best possible experience for every guest, please observe the following.

Rescheduling & Cancellations

Appointments may be cancelled or rescheduled at least 24 hours before the scheduled time. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before may result in the booking deposit being forfeited.

No-Shows

Guests who do not arrive for their appointment without prior notice may forfeit their deposit and may be required to pay a new deposit before making another booking.

Late Arrivals

Guests arriving late may receive a shortened treatment so appointments scheduled afterwards are not affected. The full treatment price may still apply.

Deposits

Selected treatments and spa packages require a booking deposit (30% of the treatment price, R100 minimum) to secure the appointment.

Group & Couples Bookings

Larger bookings and couples packages may require additional notice for cancellations or changes. Please contact us as early as possible should your plans change.
